# Independent IK baseline results

This result follows the [predeclared protocol](ik_baseline_protocol.md). The existing 97.8% arm-lab restart-enabled control was known before protocol freeze; the cold-start and independent `k` outcomes were not. All 1,000 targets and all four variant outcomes are retained. **This is full-pose, reachable-by-construction, collision-unaware kinematic evidence in simulation—not a Cartesian-uniform task distribution, runtime ranking, hardware result, or sim-to-real claim.**

## Common success results

Every success is re-evaluated with arm-lab FK at `< 1e-4 m` position error, `< 1e-3 rad` orientation error, finite values, and compiled joint limits.

The exact two-sided sign/McNemar test over the 268 discordant restart-enabled targets is `p = 8.998e-73`. It is descriptive for this one deterministic cohort, not a universal solver ranking. The maximum attempt and iteration caps are matched, but per-iteration work and early stopping are not; runtime and compute are not compared.

## Scope and retention

- Targets are generated by FK from in-limit configurations and are therefore reachable under arm-lab's exact model. They are not collision-filtered or Cartesian-uniform.
- The independent solver consumes the generated URDF. Existing tests cross-check that chain's FK against arm-lab below `1e-9`; every reported success is nevertheless rechecked through arm-lab FK.
- Alternative joint solutions are accepted when they meet the common pose and limit rule. Failure residuals are not compared because `k` restores a failed attempt's start while arm-lab returns its best attempt.
- No variant, failure, or discordant target was removed after outcomes were observed.
